Corelith is the workspace for every workflow: it sells its own products and runs its sales on its
own CRM. Members are the five directors, filling the five seats. People shown without a photo get
the person glyph until their photos are supplied.

Clock: Monday 19 October 2026, Harare. Events up to 5 October come from Corelith's own record of
the Nyatsime deal. Anything later is made up for the mock and is marked "mock" in `world.json`.

## W1. A deal worked by the team (the most frequent job, crossing people)

**Start.** Sean Muchenje, the owner of the deal, is at his laptop at 1 Beit Avenue. Gerald Pondai
is on his phone after calling the school. Tatenda Chinyamakobvu is following the deal from the
office.

**Steps**
1. **Notifications.** Needs you: Gerald mentioned Sean on *Campus for Nyatsime College* (09:58).
   Sean opens it, and the deal opens beside the list.
2. **The deal, Overview.** The comments panel is open on Gerald's comment: accounts never got the
   17 September email, because it went to an old address. Gerald is here; Tatenda is away. The
   highlights show 32 days since the proposal and 12 days left on the founding terms.
3. **Call.** Sean starts a call from Gerald's comment and brings Tatenda in. During the call he pins
   the decision: *Resend everything to the bursar today; hold the founding terms to 30 November.*
   He asks Gerald, as the person who approves terms, to approve the new date.
4. **Email the school.** A sheet rises over the deal with the email already written. It goes to the
   bursar's address that Gerald got, with the concept note, the order form and the subscription
   terms attached. Sending it logs the email as contact with the client.
5. **After.** Activity shows the email, Gerald's approval, the 12-minute call with three people,
   and the pinned decision. The follow-up "Resend the documents" ticks itself off. The terms date
   moves to 30 November.
6. **Gerald's phone.** Needs you: "Sean asked you to approve: hold the founding terms to 30
   November." Gerald approves it in one tap.

**End.** The deal is at Proposal sent with fresh documents out. The terms run to 30 November.
Sean, Gerald and Tatenda have each seen the decision. The school sees one email.

**Failures**
- The email bounces. "Not delivered: Nyatsime College's mail server refused it." Ways out: change
  the address, or send it on WhatsApp.
- Gerald's call drops on a weak line. He gets Rejoin; Sean and Tatenda carry on.
- Live presence can't connect. The deal works as normal and says "Can't show who's here".
- Sean and Gerald both edit the terms date. The second one to save sees "Gerald changed this at
  10:49", with Keep mine and Keep Gerald's.

**Actions counted.** Five clicks from the notification to the email sent, plus the call and one
tap on Gerald's phone. Nothing asks twice: sending an email is logged and can be followed up, and
the approval can be undone by Gerald.
