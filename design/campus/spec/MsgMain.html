One rule for unread, everywhere

Unread is: the last message is not mine, and I have not opened the conversation since it arrived. Derived on read, never counted into a column. Every badge on every surface — the bell, the tab, the row pip — comes from that one function. The rule is already in lib/schools/messages.ts. The three portals each invent their own badge on top of it.

Whose move it is

the same fact, said usefully
Your replyWith the familyFinished
An inbox sorted by unread says what is new. An inbox sorted by whose move it is says what is yours to do — which is what a teacher with fourteen conversations needs. Same derived value, three labels, no new column.

What a thread is not

lib/schools/messages.ts, verbatim
Not a group chat: a school’s messaging has to answer “who could read this” for a safeguarding officer a year later, and a room anybody can be added to cannot answer it.
Nothing is edited and nothing is deleted. What a parent was told has to still be true when somebody checks.
So: no group threads, no editing, no deleting. Handing a conversation on moves the staff seat and says so in the thread.
