1
Posted things are never edited in place

Nothing that reached the ledger, sub-ledger, bank or fiscal device changes. Notes, attachments and the due date change freely, logged.

2
Edit on a posted document is Revise

The old one is voided, a new one issued with a new number pointing back. The old one shows “Replaced by INV-0137”.

3
A void posts the mirror

Every direction inverted, under void:<id>, dated today or the first open day. The sub-ledger line becomes Void in the same transaction.

4
An issued invoice is voided by a full credit note

Fiscalised when the invoice was. Every reduction of output VAT sits in one place for the return.

5
Deleting a draft is free

For anyone who may edit it. Drafts carry no number, so nothing leaves a gap.

6
Deleting anything posted is owner only

Within the limits on BooksWho: never fiscalised, closed-month, filed-VAT or reconciled documents.

7
Part paid and Paid are worked out

From the balance, never set by hand. No route sets Paid.

8
Numbers are given at issue

From one gapless series per type, never at draft and never random.

9
Every action writes who and when

Void, revise, delete, reopen and override also write an audit event.

In the tables on these boards: Ledger is the journal posted; Sub-ledger the line in who owes whom; Bank the bank transaction; Fiscal FDMS; Stock the stock movement. A dashed arrow is worked out by the app, not pressed by a person.
