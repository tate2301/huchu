A QPD is 10%, 25%, 30% and 35% of the year’s estimated tax. Category A and B file every two months, C every month, D on a period the Commissioner sets. A VAT return is due on the 10th of the month after its period ends.

Exempt means no VAT charged and no input tax back. Zero-rated means VAT at 0%, and the input tax is claimed. A school charges no VAT on fees and claims none on what it buys.

Input tax

Only from valid fiscal invoices

Input tax counts only on a fiscal tax invoice that shows Valid on the FDMS portal, with the buyer’s details right. From the January 2026 periods TaRMS fills it in from FDMS.

Manual schedules, now narrow

Only imports, imported capital goods, imported services and VAT withheld still go on a hand-made schedule.

What the invoice must show

The words Fiscal Tax Invoice, both parties’ names, VAT numbers and TINs, serial number, date, currency, VAT and total, the FDMS QR code. One per supply, within 30 days; none under US$10.

To confirmTaRMS now has a VAT return line “Supply of goods or services at 10% (Exports)”. What it covers is not clear yet.

Penalties: up to US$30 a day for a late return, and 100% of unpaid VAT plus interest.

Fiscalisation

FDMS: every sale signed by ZIMRA as it happens. It is why an issued invoice can only be corrected, never changed.

Every VAT-registered business must fiscalise every sale and send it to ZIMRA’s Fiscalisation Data Management System. Fiscal memory is read-only, and no other system may record sales without approval.

Devices

Fiscal cash registers, fiscal printers, electronic signature devices for B2B invoices, or a central server that talks to FDMS by API. A fault is reported to ZIMRA within 8 hours.

Corrections

An issued invoice is never edited. A fiscalised credit or debit note corrects it: it names the original receipt, uses its currency, carries the reason (return, price, cancellation, discount, bad debt).

Penalties

US$25 for each point of sale for each day, up to 181 days. After that it is a criminal offence. FDMS compliance is a condition of the tax clearance certificate (ITF263).

To confirmReceipts are hash-chained and a day has a maximum length: from vendor API documents, as ZIMRA’s own API text could not be read. The credit note rules above are also from vendor documents.

To confirmNo official void process was found. Books treats a fiscalised document as corrected only by a credit note.

Two currencies

USD and ZiG side by side. The document’s own currency and rate are the record; everything else is a view.

Currency of the invoiceUSD
Rate27.00 ZiG to US$1
Rate fromRBZ interbank, 14 Oct 2026

Fixed when the invoice was issued. Paying it later at another rate makes a gain or loss line, never a new total.

  • The ZiG (code ZWG) came in on 5 April 2024 (SI 60 of 2024). The US dollar is still legal tender, so most firms trade in both.
  • The RBZ says the interbank market alone sets the official rate. Since SI 34 of 2025 (15 April 2025) firms may price at market rates; ICAZ accepts a credible market closing rate that can be checked.
  • Every fiscal invoice states its currency. A credit note uses the original’s currency.
  • Taxes are paid in the currency of trade, except income tax: with more than half of income in foreign currency, the firm splits it 50/50 and accounts in USD and ZWG.
  • VAT on imported services is paid in USD from 1 January 2026. NSSA is paid in ZiG at the interbank rate.
  • Many USD-led firms report in USD. Firms reporting in ZWG still apply hyperinflation accounting (IAS 29), on EY’s April 2026 view.

To confirmFinance Act 2024 s4A lets the foreign-currency part of tax be paid in ZWG at the rate on the day; commentators read the wording differently. No rule was found that forces prices in both currencies, and no current PAAB view on the ZWG.

Records

What ZIMRA can ask to see, and for how long.

Six years

Keep records for at least 6 years (Income Tax Act s37B, VAT Act s57). Not keeping them is an offence.

English, and retrievable

Books in English. Electronic records are fine if ZIMRA officers can reach them and get them back in their original form.

Issued is final

Fiscal memory is read-only and one fiscal invoice exists per supply, so an issued document is corrected, never edited or deleted.

Serial numbers

The law asks for an individual serial number on each invoice; FDMS counters make the run auditable.

To confirmNo explicit “no gaps” rule was found; Books numbers without gaps anyway. Public companies must be audited and private ones are exempt; check against the Companies and Other Business Entities Act (2020).

How small firms actually work

Practice, not law: the channels, the controls and who does the books at month end.

EcoCashInnBucksOneMoneyZIPITRTGS in ZiGRTGS in USD (Nostro FCA)Card swipeUSD cash
Mobile money first

About 87% of electronic payments by volume in Q1 2026 went through mobile money. Each wallet is an account to reconcile, in each currency.

Cash, counted daily

US dollar cash is still common. A daily cash-up against the fiscal day’s Z report, and the takings banked.

Petty cash and requisitions

An imprest float with petty cash vouchers. Spending starts as a requisition: approved by someone else, paid out, then acquitted with the slips.

Buying

Requisition, purchase order, goods received, the supplier’s fiscal invoice, then payment. Check their ITF263 and that the invoice shows Valid.

Selling

Quotation, fiscal invoice, then the payment allocated. Contract jobs, flooring among them, take deposits, which raise time-of-supply questions.

Month end with the accountant

A bookkeeper or an outside accountant closes the month: bank and wallet reconciliations, VAT working schedule, journals in Pastel or Sage, or in a spreadsheet.

To confirmAccountants here are mostly trained on Sage Pastel, with QuickBooks and spreadsheets common in small firms. This is practice we have seen, not from the sourced brief.

Pay ZIMRA by deposit or transfer against the TIN; a payment is not allocated until its return is filed. The tax year is the calendar year.

What this means for Books

Eight rules the Books core and its screens follow. Breaking one is a defect.

01
Issued is final

A fiscalised invoice, a posted payment, a closed month: never edited or deleted. A correction is a new document (credit note, debit note, reversing journal) linked to the original. Only drafts can be deleted. Everything keeps its history for 6 years.

02
Each document keeps its own currency

USD or ZiG, the rate, and where the rate came from, fixed at issue. Nothing converts in place; reports convert and show the gain or loss.

03
The calendar leads

Money opens on what is due and how much: VAT by the 15th, PAYE by the 10th, the next QPD. The dates sit in one table, because ZIMRA has moved them twice since 2025.

04
Fiscalise at issue

A sale is done when FDMS has signed it. Device and fiscal-day errors show where the owner looks, not in a log.

05
Only valid invoices claim VAT

Input tax counts once the supplier’s fiscal invoice shows Valid. The supplier record carries its ITF263 and warns of the 30% before anyone pays.

06
Money by channel

Every cash box, wallet and bank account, in each currency, is its own account with its own reconciliation. IMTT is a separate charge, never netted away.

07
Plain on top, double entry underneath

The owner reads money in, money out, owed to you and you owe. The accountant gets journals, the trial balance, the GL and period close, with the same figures, laid out the way Pastel taught them.

08
Two people where money leaves

Requisitions, supplier payments, credit notes and pay runs need a second person. Nobody approves their own, and nobody both approves and pays.
