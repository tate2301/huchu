Interactions

How a card reads, what happens when someone presses it or sets it aside, how drafts reach a person, and how to hand Vane a job.

A card

Every card in For you, the panel and inside modules.

Issue · Books · 06:043 invoices are past their terms, $2,480.00.Chisipite Dental is 12 days late, later than they usually pay. Let’s open their statements and call them first. What I read: Invoices, Owed to us by age · 3 rows
1Kind and source
Issue or Suggestion, the module, the time. Issues use the warning colour on the icon only.
2One fact
Names, amounts with both decimals, dates. No adjectives.
3The reason, then “Let’s …”
One or two sentences in the first person.
4The button names the act
Never “OK” or “Do it”. The primary shows its shortcut; S on the focused card.
5What I read
The report templates and the question Vane ran, opened as the same view.
6Not now
Moves it to Muted and asks why, in one tap: not mine, already done, wrong, not now.

A card’s life

OpenIn For you and the panel, counted on the button.
PressedThe person presses the act. The card shows who did what, then goes to Done.
Resolved on its ownIf the thing is fixed elsewhere (the invoice is paid), Vane closes the card and says why.
Not nowMuted, with the reason. Three of a kind muted: Vane asks to stop that kind.

A draft’s life

Invoices, orders, reminders, work orders, report blocks, form changes.

Vane draftsA draft row in its module, marked “Vane’s draft”, with what it read.
The person opens itChanges anything. The mark stays until it is issued or deleted.
Issue, send, approveThe app’s own button and endpoint. The audit reads “by Rudo, drafted by Vane”.

Give Vane a job

Give Vane a job“Get September’s VAT ready.” Ctrl J, from any page.
Vane proposesAn answer, or a plan with steps marked Vane or You, and what it will read.
StartNothing runs before Start. Vane does its steps; yours wait in Plans.

Vane’s states

The four the handover left undrawn are drawn on the pages to the right.

WorkingPulsing dot on the button; the job’s label and progress, “38 of 61 accounts”.
PausedFrom Pause Vane. Nothing runs; cards stay. The button shows Paused.
Quiet hours20:00 to 06:00 by default. Work goes on; cards wait for the morning.
Over the allowanceJobs this month are used up. Cards already made stay; new jobs start on the 1st.
Could not work it outThe card says what it tried and offers Show the rows. No guess.
Cannot reach the serviceRetries on its own; shows when it last worked.
