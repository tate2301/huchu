VAT withholding agents and undo

Two rarer cases, and how each step is taken back.

Our customer withholds VAT

Their payment is short by a third of the VAT. Record the receipt with VAT withheld by the customer and the certificate number: Dr 1170 VAT withheld receivable, which the VAT return claims.

We withhold VAT

If we are an agent (a setting in Money and tax), our supplier payments withhold a third of the VAT the same way, to 2185 VAT withheld payable.

Undo, at each point

Requisition or PO before money moves: cancel. A goods receipt: reverse. A bill: void or revise. A payment: void, and the bank line reopens.
