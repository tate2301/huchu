Stock, maintenance, gold and schools

Posting through the drain and the core’s Issue.

Stock valuation

Movements already post. Adjustments captured Pending today post through the drain. Over adjustmentPinOver, or a count difference over countOwnerOver, needs approval first.

Work order completed

Posts parts and labour: Dr maintenance expense by the asset’s cost centre, Cr stock and accrued labour or the supplier bill. Status changes use recordEvent. Reopening voids the completion posting.

Gold

Purchases and buyer receipts post now. Dispatches, allocations and corrections post through the drain. Settlements post as a payroll disbursement. Billing goes through the core’s Issue.

Schools

Fee documents keep posting and gain sub-ledger lines, so a guardian’s balance and Books’ aging agree. Fees are VAT exempt.
