Templates from Corelith

The starting points every workspace gets. A template is a saved way of reading a report; a built report puts several together on one page. Nobody changes Corelith’s: they make it theirs.

What a template holds

NameSays what it reads, in plain words: “Takings by payment”, not “Payments report v2”.
What it showsOne line, shown in the catalogue. Every Corelith template has one.
ReadsA report key, or several for a built report. Reading needs the module, the role and the campus.
Opens onThe dates it starts with. Saved templates keep their dates only if asked.
Narrowed bySite, status, till, class, cashier: the report’s own choices, plus filters.
One row for eachGroup by, or roll up to one row per product, day, till, cashier or class.
Columns and totalsWhich show, in what order, and which are summed, averaged or counted.
PageFigures, charts, a breakdown and the table. Managers arrange it.
Seen byJust me, managers or everyone.
Made byCorelith, your team or you. Corelith’s never change; Make it yours copies one.

Three kinds

ReportOne report with a saved view: the table, and the page above it.
BuiltBlocks whose questions read one or more reports: figures, charts, tables, text.
StatementBooks and Payroll statements. Listed here, opened in their module.
