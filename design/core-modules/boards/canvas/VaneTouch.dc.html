Where people meet Vane

Vane lives inside the screens people already use. It never opens a separate chat; it speaks where the work is, in the workspace’s own components.

1 · The top bar

On every page: the mark, the count of open items for this person, and a dot that pulses while Vane is working. Ctrl J opens the panel.

2 · The panel

Opens on the right of any module, 400 px, and shows this module’s items first. Closing it never loses anything.

  • For you: issues and suggestions for this module, then the rest

  • Give Vane a job: a plain request, answered with a plan or a draft

3 · Beside the record

In a table, a row Vane has something on carries a short flag in its own column. In a record, the note sits above the section it concerns.

4 · In a form

When Vane knows a likely answer, it shows it in the box, greyed. Tab takes it; typing replaces it. It never fills a box silently.

5 · Drafts

A draft Vane made opens in the normal screen, marked at the top, so the person edits and sends it like any other.

6 · Outside the workspace

At 07:00, people who asked for it get one message with what Vane found overnight. Each line opens the item. Quiet hours hold the rest.
