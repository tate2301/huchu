Vane

How Vane works

Vane is part of every Corelith workspace, not a separate app. It works all day in the background, finds what needs a person, and gets the work ready. People decide and press the button.

The loop

Five steps, running for every workspace on its own queue.

Watches

New and changed records in every product the workspace runs, a check every few minutes, and a nightly run at 06:00.

Works it out

Reads through a read-only SQL role, compares with the workspace’s own habits, and keeps the SQL it ran.

Raises and drafts

Writes an issue or a suggestion, drafts the invoice, reminder or payslip change, or breaks a bigger job into a plan.

A person acts

Sends, approves, pays or changes, with the same buttons and shortcuts as the rest of the workspace.

Closes the loop

Marks the item done, notes what was taken and what was muted, and learns which suggestions this workspace wants.

What it does on its own, and what waits

Enforced on the server. Vane’s tools can only write drafts and its own items.

On its own
  • Read what the workspace allows
  • Work out figures and compare with habits
  • Draft invoices, reminders, payslip changes, report questions
  • Propose bank matches and leave cover
  • Break a job into a plan and do its read-only steps
  • Raise issues and suggestions
Waits for a person
  • Send a message, reminder or quote
  • Approve leave or a pay run
  • Pay anyone, or record a payment
  • Accept a bank match
  • Change a record or a salary
  • File a return with ZIMRA or NSSA

An item’s life

Every issue and suggestion is one row in vane_items, with the SQL it ran and what it read.
