Ask, build or write: one question

Explore is the studio for people who want the workspace’s data itself. Reports, insights and Vane share its engine: however a question starts, in plain words, as steps or as SQL, it is stored as SQL and its rows. Everything else is a view over those two.

Power without risk: Explore can read a lot, and change nothing.

Who can open ExploreOwners, managers and anyone Management lets in. Everyone else asks Vane or uses reports.
What it readsThe tables the person’s role reads in the app, and only this workspace’s rows. People tables stay with People managers.
How it readsAs explore_reader: select and with only. A change, a delete or a second statement is refused before it runs.
How much5,000 rows back and 10 seconds per question. Every question run is kept in Management → Explore, with who ran it.

Where suggestions come from

Vane watches how people use the workspace and offers to save them the trip.

You export the same list every week→Schedule it to the person who needs it
You sort or filter by a figure the list doesn't show→Add it as a column
You open records one by one to compare them→A question that compares them in one table
A figure moved more than usual→An insight, with the rows behind it
You ask Vane the same thing twice→Save it as a question, or pin it to a board
A question's result crossed a line before→Watch it and raise an issue next time

Where it shows up

Its own place on the rail, and inside the screens people already use.

  • Explore, on the rail: the tables, the ask box, steps beside SQL, rows below. Every panel resizes.
  • Every worksheet: Explore this list, which opens the list’s own SQL
  • The toolbar’s Vane slot: suggestions for the list on screen
  • Ctrl K: ask a question anywhere and get the figure back, with Open in Explore
  • Reports: a block on a board is a saved question; Edit opens it here
