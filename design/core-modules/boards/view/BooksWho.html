Permanent delete and edit after posting

Both are owner or superuser only, always with a reason, always in the audit trail.

Edit after posting is Revise

Never an edit in place. The old document is voided and a new one issued with a new number, linked back. Both stay visible: “Replaced by INV-0137”. Notes, attachments and the due date change freely, logged.

Permanent delete, owner only

With a reason of 3 to 300 characters. In one transaction it removes the document, its lines, every journal it posted, its sub-ledger lines and events. Payments stay, as the party’s money on account. The number is never reused.

Always in the audit trail

A books.<document>.deleted or .revised event keeps the whole record, who, why, and for a superuser the operator and the session (SA-214).

Why fiscalised documents are refused: if we deleted the invoice, our books would show less output VAT than FDMS holds, and since 2026 the buyer’s input tax fills in from FDMS, so ZIMRA would see a sale we no longer declare. This changes today’s delete, which removes a fiscalised invoice once its fiscal day has closed. The one exception: documents signed on a device registered against ZIMRA’s test environment, purged by the owner with the test data.

It was fiscalised on 2 Oct and FDMS shows it Valid. ZIMRA allows a fiscalised invoice to be corrected only by a fiscal credit note. Chisipite Dental’s input tax already shows it.
