The cost tracker

Each kind of entry goes where it belongs. The gap the CRM finance page shows today closes.

Spent, from a requisition

A line of that requisition’s acquittal. Posts with the acquittal.

Spent, own pocket

Becomes a claim line when the day is sent. Approve, then reimburse.

Spent, from a float

A petty cash voucher against the person’s float.

Money received on site

Must name the invoice or customer. A receipt into the rep’s cash float: the cash is with the rep until banked.

Deleting an entry

Before the day is sent, it removes the draft. After, refused; void the document it fed.
