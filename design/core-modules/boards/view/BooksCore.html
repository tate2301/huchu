Books

Books is the engine

Every money movement in every module goes through one core. The core is free and always on; the Books screens are what a workspace pays for. A workspace without the screens still has correct books, from its first day.

One entry point

CRM, retail, stock, maintenance, payroll, schools and gold call lib/books/core. The core writes the ledger, the sub-ledgers and the bank, works out tax, and signs with the fiscal device.

  1. Capturing an event defaults to Pending, not Ignored.
  2. Ignored is allowed only through recordEvent, which refuses any amount.
  3. A missing rule or account makes the event Failed, with the reason. It is never dropped.
  4. One drain runs every 15 minutes for every source type, backing off from 5 minutes, doubling, up to 24 hours.
  5. Pending over an hour and every Failed event show in Close the month › Needs posting, and block the close.
  6. A one-off backfill re-queues every past Ignored event that carries an amount.
