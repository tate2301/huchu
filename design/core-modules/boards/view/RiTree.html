Reports & Insights · every workspace

The tree

Reports & Insights is one module in every workspace. Insights answers a question in one page. All reports is the catalogue of templates: Corelith’s, your team’s and yours.

The tree

One module. Routes are the app’s, with two new: the Overview and /reports/all.

Reportsrail · every workspaceShows when the person can read at least one report. Same pages in every vertical; retail wears the Tender look.
Overview/reportsWhat changed since you last looked: each Insights headline, the templates opened most, and how each module is doing.
Insights/reports/insights/[topic]One page per topic, in one shape: a headline written from the figures, four figures against the period before, one question with one chart, tables in tabs, and Do something about it.
Topicsset by the workspaceSales and CRM: Sales, Site visits, Team. Retail: Sales, Profit, Products, Stock health, Losses, Customers, Money. Schools: Fees, Attendance, Enrolment, Boarding. Gold mine: Production, Downtime, Gold, Stores. Payroll: Pay cost, Statutory. Workshop: Equipment, Stores.
All reports/reports/allEvery template this person can open, grouped by area. Tabs: All, From Corelith, Made by your team, Just yours.
Area/reports/all?area=[slug]The same list, one area. The workspace’s own areas come first; Pay, People and Books come last everywhere.
A report/reports/[key]Dates and the report’s own choices, search, filters, group by, columns. The page: figures, charts, breakdown, table. Save as a template, Export, Send it.
Arrange the page/reports/[key]/arrangeManagers only. Blocks: headline figures, bar chart, trend, breakdown, heading, text, the table.
Save as a templatesheetName, what it shows, seen by (just me, managers, everyone), keep the dates.
Export and Send itsheetPDF in five layouts, Excel workbook, CSV, all or selected rows. Send it on a schedule.
A template/reports/[key]?template=[id]A saved view of one report: its columns, filters, grouping, totals and dates.
A built report/reports/built/[key] · /reports/custom/[id]Blocks whose questions read one or more reports. From Corelith: Make it yours. Yours: Edit opens the builder.
Edit, in the builder/reports/custom/[id]/editThe same builder as forms and documents: outline, page, inspector. Questions in Steps or SQL.
From Books and Payrollopens in its moduleProfit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, trial balance, ageing, VAT, P2 and NSSA are listed here and open where they live.
SettingsManagement → ReportsManagers: which reports are offered, everyone’s starting view and each report’s page.
