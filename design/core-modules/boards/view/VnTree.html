Vane · every workspace

The tree

Vane is one module in every workspace and a panel beside every page. It reads what the person could read, writes cards, plans and drafts, and waits for a person to press the button.

Routes are proposed; nothing of Vane exists in the app yet.

How a job runs

The same five steps for every template.

Something changesA record is saved, a schedule comes round (every few minutes, 06:00, Monday), or someone asks.
A jobOne per workspace, kind, subject and day, so it never runs twice.
Vane readsThrough report templates, as the person it is for. Read only.
Vane writesA card, a plan, or a draft marked as Vane’s. Nothing else.
A person pressesSend, issue, approve, keep, publish, file: the app’s own buttons.
