Vane’s templates

Every job Vane does starts from a template: when it runs, which report templates it reads, what it may write, and the one button a person presses. Forms and documents meet it through drafts that wait to be kept.

What a Vane template holds

KindIssue (something is wrong), Suggestion (something to do), Plan (work in steps), Draft (a record or a block, marked as Vane’s), Answer (to a question someone asked).
WhenA record change, a schedule, or someone asking. Quiet hours hold the telling, not the work.
ReadsReport templates, never tables. If the person cannot open the report, Vane cannot read it for them.
Does on its ownReading, working out, comparing with habits, drafting, planning. Nothing a customer, a bank or ZIMRA would see.
The person pressesOne button that names the act, with its shortcut. It calls the same endpoint as the app’s own button.
Lands inA card in For you, a plan, or a draft in its module. Drafts carry “Vane’s draft” until someone changes or issues them.
Routed toA role or a person, from Management → Vane. Fitters get only their own visits and quotes.

How it meets reports and forms

Reports: Vane reads through templates
Report templateVane reads Quotes, opened 7 days, every owner: the same view a person would open.
Card“16 quotes have had no reply for 7 days.” What I read opens that exact view.
Save as a templateIf people open the view every week, Vane names it and suggests saving it.
Reports: a block in the builder
Ask in the builder“Add won m² by month.”
Vane’s blockThe question in Steps, or SQL when it needs a join, with its rows. An accent line marks it.
KeepThe block becomes yours. Not now removes it. Saving the report is still Save.
Forms: changes from the answers
Answers come inSite visit answers, read weekly per form.
Vane’s change“Make Subfloor required”, “Add Screed as an option”, “Underlay factor 1.15”. Drafted, with the answers behind it.
Keep, then PublishKept changes join the form’s draft. Reps see them only once someone publishes.
Documents: checks and the message
Document draftedA quote from a visit, an invoice from a quote.
Vane checks and writesLines against the price list; the WhatsApp or email text that goes with it.
Change the line · SendEach change and the send are the person’s.
