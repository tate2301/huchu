Explore

Explore: the studio for power users, with guarded access to the workspace’s data

Explore from other screens, and who may use it

Explore has its own place on the rail, between Reports and Documents, for power users.

Every panel resizes: drag the tables list’s edge, the line between Steps and SQL, and the editor’s foot to give the rows more room.

Guarded: questions run as explore_reader (select and with only), see only the tables the person’s role reads in the app and only this workspace’s rows, return at most 5,000 rows and stop at 10 s. A change is refused before it runs. Management → Explore sets who may use it.

Human resources

Payroll

People holds payroll, as in the reference: Everyone, Time off, Payroll, Loans and advances, Documents, Starting and leaving.

Not in the app yet: · a page per person (the schools staff list already links to /people/{id}, which is missing) · approving or rejecting leave (the leave API only creates requests).

Not in the app yet: the phone payslip (payslips are a PDF today), and a bank or EcoCash payment file.

Build: forms, documents, reports and templates

One builder for all of them

The form, document and report builders share one frame. The outline and the inspector resize from their edges (drag the grip at the panel’s foot), and the canvas takes what is left; each person’s widths are kept.

Templates now live in Build, not Management. The list joins the Template Library (/templates), document layouts and intake forms, so a template is one row however it is used.

Using a template makes your own copy. Forms, documents, emails, reports and templates all open in the same builder: the bar, the outline with Add (/), the page, and the inspector.
