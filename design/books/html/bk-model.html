The lifecycle

Every document. Numbers are given at issue from a gapless series, never at draft.

DraftIssuedPart paidPaid
DeleteDraft only, anyone who may edit it. No number, so no gap.
VoidPosts the mirror, dated in an open month. Sales invoices void through a full credit note.
ReviseVoid plus a new document with a new number. The old one reads "Replaced by INV-0145".
Delete postedOwner or outside accountant, with a reason. Refused once fiscalised, in a closed month, in a filed return or a closed reconciliation.
