Corelith Books

Books handles every dollar in the workspace. Its screens are what you pay for.

The core posts every sale, payment, payout and pay run from every module, from the first day, free. The screens at /books are the paid way to see and run it: invoices and bills in one place, bank matching, VAT, the month's close, and the ledger for the accountant. An owner can run it without an accounting degree; an accountant can be handed it.

Density, from Ström-Awn's Config 2026 talk

Density is what a person gets done per minute and per pixel, not how much is on the screen.

ValueMoney answers "am I all right, and what needs me" inside 1440 x 900 without a scroll.
InformationNo icon repeating its column. Cents muted. Currency once in the header. Status is a dot and a word.
DesignProximity and alignment group things; one tray per region, never a card per item. Rows carry counts, not paragraphs.
TemporalRecords open beside the list first. Every primary action has a key. J and K move. Waits over 10 seconds show a count.
Two densitiesOwner's path at 40px rows and sentences. Accountant's path (Ledger, Tax, Close) at 32px rows with codes.
ChromeThe finding is one 20px line. Section heads sit 16px from what they follow. About 120px a screen goes back to rows.

What one month looks like

Floorcode, a flooring firm in Harare running CRM, Tender at the showroom and Payroll for its fitters.

  1. 1SellQuote, invoice, fiscalise, take the money. Reps take cash on site into their float.
  2. 2SpendRequisitions, bills from fiscal tax invoices, supplier payments with withholding checked.
  3. 3BankCBZ statement weekly. Vane proposes matches. Reconcile each account.
  4. 4Pay peoplePayroll posts the run. PAYE, NSSA and ZIMDEF show as due in Tax.
  5. 5TaxVAT 7 return by the 10th, paid by the 15th. Withholding by the 5th and 10th.
  6. 6CloseChecklist green, revaluation posted, month closed. The accountant exports.
