# Shortcuts

Every primary action has a key, shown on its button, and the key works. Keys act on the page in view and never fire while typing in a field (except Ctrl and Esc combinations). On every list: J and K move, Space selects, Enter opens, Esc clears the selection. Ctrl K opens search everywhere; / focuses the panel search.

Taken from the screens' markup. Where one key means different things on different pages, the page decides.
