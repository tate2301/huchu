# Getting your Facebook leads into the CRM

When someone fills in the form on your Facebook or Instagram ad, their details
appear in your CRM within seconds — already tagged with the ad and campaign that
brought them in. No spreadsheet, no downloading leads from Facebook every
morning.

**Time:** about five minutes.

---

## Before you start

One thing only: you need to be an **admin** of the Facebook Page your ads run
from.

Not sure? Open your Page → **Settings** → **Page access**, find your name, and
check it says **full control**. If it doesn't, ask whoever owns the Page to give
it to you first. Nothing below works until that's true.

---

## Connect your Page

**1. Open CRM → Settings → Facebook ads**

**2. Press "Connect Facebook"**

You'll be taken to Facebook.

**3. Sign in and approve**

Facebook asks whether you allow the CRM to see your Pages and read leads from
them. Press **Continue**.

If Facebook offers a list of Pages here, tick the one your ads run from. If you
only have one, it will be picked for you.

**4. Choose your Page**

You'll come back to the CRM and see your Pages listed. Click the one you want.

That's it. The Page appears in your list with a green dot, and leads start
arriving from the next form someone fills in.

---

## Check it worked

Send yourself a test lead:

1. Go to **developers.facebook.com/tools/lead-ads-testing**
2. Choose your Page and your lead form
3. Press **Create lead**

Open **CRM → Leads**. Your test should be at the top, in the **New** stage,
within a few seconds.

> Test leads are real records. Archive the test one when you're done, or it will
> sit in your pipeline looking like a customer nobody has called.

---

## Reading the Facebook ads screen

Each connected Page shows a coloured dot:

| Dot | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Green | Working. Leads are arriving. |
| 🟠 Orange | Connected, but Facebook hasn't confirmed it yet. Press **Test**. |
| 🔴 Red | Something's wrong. The screen says what, with a **Reconnect** button. |

**Leads** opens the list of everything Facebook has sent for that Page:

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ingested** | A lead was created. Nothing to do. |
| **duplicate** | Facebook sent the same lead twice. Already handled. |
| **ignored** | A lead from a form you've excluded, or while the Page was paused. |
| **failed** | Something broke. There's a **Retry** button and a reason. |

---

## If something goes wrong

**"I sent a test lead and nothing showed up."**
Press **Test** on the Page. It asks Facebook to confirm it's still sending leads
here, and usually says exactly what's wrong.

**"Leads were arriving, then stopped."**
Your Facebook access expired — most often after a password change, or when
someone removed the app from the Page. Press **Reconnect this Page**, sign in
again, then press **Retry** on any failed leads.

**"Only my own test leads come through — real customers don't."**
Tell your support contact. The CRM's Facebook app needs Meta's approval before
it can read leads from the public, and that's on our side, not yours.

**"Leads arrive with the name 'Facebook lead'."**
Your ad's form doesn't ask for a name. The email or phone number is still
captured, so you can still call them — but it's worth editing the form on
Facebook to ask.

**"It says my Page is already connected to another workspace."**
A Facebook Page can only send its leads to one place. Whoever connected it needs
to disconnect it there first.

---

## Two things worth knowing

**Facebook keeps a lead's answers for 90 days.** If you see red on the Facebook
ads screen, deal with it that month — a lead retried after 90 days comes back
empty.

**Pausing a Page doesn't queue leads.** While a Page is paused, its leads stay
on Facebook and never reach the CRM. Pause is for stopping, not for holding.
