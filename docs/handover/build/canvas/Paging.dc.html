The decision

Pages, with Back to top

People open these lists to find a record, check a set and reconcile it, not to browse. Infinite scroll is built for browsing a feed, and usability research has said so for years. On a worksheet it also takes away the footer, and the footer is where the totals live.

How the pager behaves

  1. 50 rows by default, with 25, 50 or 100 to choose. Each list remembers your choice.
  2. Page, rows per page, sort, filters and grouping are in the address. Open a shift and come back, and you land on the same page.
  3. Changing a filter goes back to page 1.
  4. Changing page scrolls the table, not the window, back to its first row.
  5. Back to top appears after one screen of scrolling, in the pager's right-hand slot, so the page buttons never move.

Selection and totals across pages

  1. Ticked rows stay ticked when you change page, up to 500.
  2. "Select all 312" selects what the filters let through, not only this page, and says so.
  3. Totals always cover the whole filtered set. They come from the server, not from adding up the 50 rows on screen.
  4. On a phone the pager shrinks to ‹ 2 of 7 › and the totals shrink to one line.
  5. Keyboard: J and K move between rows, Space ticks, Enter opens.
