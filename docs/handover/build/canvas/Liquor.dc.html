W-61 · Shop-type features

A liquor store, switched on

What changes when Management › Company › Business type is Liquor store. Each feature is on by default for that type, off for General retail, and can be turned off on its own. The till reads the switches; nothing here is a separate app.

1 · Till

Age check at the till

Categories carry a Check ID switch, set on for beer, spirits, wine and ciders when the shop type is chosen. The till asks once a sale, before payment, and the answer is kept with the sale.

2 · Till

Licence trading hours

The licence hours set in Company stop age-restricted categories selling outside them. Everything else still sells. A manager PIN cannot override it; the licence is the law.

3 · Till and admin

Empties and deposits

Returnable products carry a deposit. The till charges it, takes bottles back for cash or off the sale, and the Empties ledger tracks what is in the store and what each supplier owes for returned crates.

4 · Till and admin

Cases and singles

A case is its own product linked to its single. Stock is counted in singles, so cases and singles never disagree. The till opens a case when singles run out, or a manager breaks one from the product.
