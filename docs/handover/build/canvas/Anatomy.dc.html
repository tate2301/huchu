Six bands, each with one job

  1. 1App bar, 48. The page's name and its one primary action. Nothing about the rows lives here.
  2. 2Toolbar, 48 — the same height as the app bar. Read left to right as one sentence: find (search), narrow (filters, each showing its label and its answer), then the count and Clear, then how the rows are shown (sort, group, columns) and what leaves the page (export). Only the main filters sit on the row; the rest live in Filters, whose badge counts the ones in use. When the row runs out of room, controls fold into menus instead of wrapping or spilling: sort, group and columns into View, then the main filters into Filters. Ticked rows turn the row into the selection's actions, and those fold into ⋯ the same way.
  3. 3Column head, 34. On the grey ground, sentence case, never capitals. The sorted column is in ink with its arrow; the rest are muted. Pinned under the toolbar.
  4. 4Group heading, 36, when grouped. The group's count and its own subtotals, in the same columns as the figures under them. Pinned under the column head while its rows pass.
  5. 5Rows, 40 (44 on touch). One line each. The reference is the link; the rest of the row can be selected and copied. A cell is drawn as what its value is, so exceptions stand out without anyone reading every row.
  6. 6Totals, pinned to the bottom of the rows. They sum everything the filters let through, not just this page, and sit in the same columns as the figures above them, to the pixel. Tick rows and a line above sums those too, like a spreadsheet's status bar. This is the only band with a shadow, because rows run underneath it.
  7. 7Pager, 48. Where you are and how much there is on the left, pages in the middle, Back to top on the right once you have scrolled past a screen. It closes the frame at the same height as the bar that opens it.
  8. Depth without decoration. White for the rows, the grey ground for the strips that frame them, a darker plate for group headings. That is what stops the page reading flat.
