Art direction

Real shops, drawn as a street

Tender is drawn as the places it runs: the counter, the till, the scale, the shop front. Isometric, quiet, and specific enough that a shopkeeper in Mbare recognises the room.

One accent per drawing

Forms are blue-grey on a warm cream ground. Tomato marks the one thing the drawing is about: the sale header, the total, an awning. Marigold and EcoCash blue appear only where they are true to the object.

Labels are live text

The pills (a product's mark tile and name, or a shop's name) are an HTML layer on top of the drawing, never baked in. They translate, scale and stay sharp. A hairline and a ring tie each pill to the thing it names.

Specific, never stock

Real figures (US$21.68, Sale #31862, Tomatoes 1.40 kg), real kinds of shop, real Harare places. No people's faces, no character art, no generic devices.

Scenes

Shop fronts

Six fronts in all: grocery, liquor, salon, hardware, clothing, wholesale. Pick the one that matches the workspace's kind of shop, so the first screen a new shop sees looks like its own.

Devices

Devices show a real till screen. One file each works on light and dark grounds.

Illustrated icons

Thirty-odd isometric icons, 1024 px, light and dark. They illustrate; they are not interface icons. Buttons, rows and menus use the solid icon set.

Where drawings go in the product

YesEmpty states (an empty Shifts list gets the shift icon), first run and setup, sign-in, offline and error pages, the POS idle screen, printed and emailed documents.
NoInside tables, toolbars, menus or dialogs on a working screen. Never two drawings on one screen. Never a light export on a dark ground, or the other way round.
