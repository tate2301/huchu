Design system

Tender in the product

The same components as every Corelith product, answered in Tender's colours. Orange is spent once per screen; everything else is warm ink on warm greys.

Orange once

The primary action is the only orange fill on a screen. The brighter orange marks focus and ticks. Nothing else is orange: not selection, not active filters, not headings.

States are not the brand

Short is crimson, over and unfinished are amber, running is indigo, balanced is a hollow circle. Each carries a word or a sign, so colour is never the only signal.

Dark by layering

Dark mode is warm near-black with each surface a step lighter, not a stack of shadows. Accents and status colours lift a step to keep their contrast.

Actions

Primary: #B84A0C under a white label, 5.2:1. Several actions in a row become one group. A destructive action names its object and stays an outline until the confirm.
