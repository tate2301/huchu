Retail & POS

POS & checkout, stock control, shift management, purchasing, reporting. Five modules for retail operations from checkout to close of day, across branches.

Vertical kit 5 modules 5 pages Production /verticals/retail
Modules

What this kit covers

Five modules — branch ops in one place; POS itself runs in the POS portal.

01
POS & checkout
Configuration of tills, products, pricing rules, promotions.
02
Stock control
Branch stockpile, low-stock alerts, expiry, shrink reports.
03
Shift management
Schedules, cashier assignment, shift open/close & cash-up.
04
Purchasing
Supplier orders, GRN receiving, returns and credit notes.
05
Reporting
Daily takings, basket size, top SKUs, branch comparison.
